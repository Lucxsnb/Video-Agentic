# Zombie Outbreak — series bible

## Format
- Vertical mini-series, ~3 min per episode (user preference: 2:50–3:00), English narration (3rd person) + short dialogue.
- Preset `dark_miniseries` (tall body rig, blurred sets, heavy vignette/grain), music mood `tense`.
- Badge "ZOMBIE OUTBREAK · EP n" + in-story clock. Every episode ends with a 2-option poll; badge = next day.
- Opening: 1-line hook with the day number; next episodes recap the winning poll option in the first lines.

## Places
- **FreshMart** supermarket (invented brand): aisle (`supermarket_aisle`), stockroom (`storage_room`),
  back area with the walk-in **cold storage** (`cold_storage`, sticker "DO NOT OPEN"), glass front doors
  (`glass_doors` prop over `street_night`, sign "CLOSED").
- Mom lives four blocks away.

## Rules / running gags
- RULE #1 (Nina): never open the cold storage — locked since day one.
- Mr. Hollis keeps the hammer close "just in case"; he suspects the courier's bite.
- The courier keeps saying "it's just a scratch".
- Thirteen days of canned beans.
- The outbreak reached the city on DAY 4 (the courier promised to be home "on Tuesday").
